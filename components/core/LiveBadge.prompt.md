Rounded red pill with a blinking dot, for live or interactive things (the AI audit, a "now booking" note).
```jsx
<LiveBadge>Interactive diagnostic engine</LiveBadge>
<LiveBadge dot={false}>Free · 3 minutes</LiveBadge>
```
- Use at most one per screen, above the title of the thing it marks. Not a status chip: there are no success/warning colours.
