Red caps eyebrow with a leading hairline; `Tag` is the rule-less micro version for card categories.
```jsx
<SectionLabel variant="hero">Business growth partners · South Africa</SectionLabel>
<SectionLabel>What we do</SectionLabel>
<SectionLabel align="center">How we work</SectionLabel>
<Tag>Automation</Tag>
```
- 2–5 words, written in sentence case (CSS uppercases). Use " · " to join two ideas.
- Always directly above a Playfair title; never on its own. `SectionHeading` already includes one.
- Red text at 0.7rem is 3.6:1 on black — decorative label only; don't put essential information here.
