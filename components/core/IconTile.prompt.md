Dark rounded square for one icon or one greyscaled emoji, placed beside or above a title.
```jsx
<IconTile icon="layers" />
<IconTile emoji="⚡" size="lg" />
<IconTile emoji="🚚" active />
```
- The site leads feature cards ("Fast implementation", "No lock-in contracts") and audit options with emoji; the tile greys them out so red stays the only accent. Prefer `icon` for anything new.
