01 · Brand

The Logo

A clean vector master: Montserrat letterforms rebuilt on the original positions, and the custom slashed D vectorised.

01 · Brand

Voice & Imagery

Honest about being new, hands-on, South African. Real, warm-lit photography of people at work.

02 · Colour

Black, and Signal Red

One dark theme. Red is a signal, not a field — buttons, labels, numbers, active states and one full-red call-to-action band.

03 · Typography

Playfair & Inter

A heavy serif headline whose last beat turns red and italic, framed by tiny, widely tracked Inter caps.

04 · Layout & effects

Space, Depth & Motion

A 1200px column, 120px sections, tight corners; black shadows for lift, red light for emphasis.

05 · Iconography

45 Line Icons

Thin strokes that take their colour from the text around them. They replace the site's emoji, so the only colour stays red.

06 · Components

Home Hero & Marquee

Grid, drifting red light, a Playfair 900 headline with a red-italic beat, and the scrolling service band.

06 · Components

Process & Call to Action

Four numbered steps on the raised band; the one full-red band closes every page.

06 · Components

Inner Pages & Service Detail

Breadcrumb, label and two-beat headline; each service as a numbered row with its icon and deliverables.

06 · Components

Navigation & Footer

Nav bar with the red draw-in underline, WhatsApp button, the sticky call-to-action bar, and the footer.

06 · Components

Core & Cards

Buttons with the bright-red sweep, labels, badges, icon tiles; dark hairline cards that lift with a red sheen.

06 · Components

Services, Stats & Insights

The hairline service grid, stat strips, blog cards and the audit's result metrics.

06 · Components

Forms & Feedback

The AI audit's step header, option tiles and pills; contact fields; alerts, toast and modal.

06 · Components

FAQ, Testimonials & Logo

FAQ answers restate the site only. Testimonial copy is placeholder — publish real, permitted quotes only.

07 · Social & blog

Share Image & Blog Covers

1200 × 630 — link previews on LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Facebook, and a cover for each article.

07 · Social & blog

Feed Posts & Story

Square, portrait and story formats in the same system — every word comes from the site.

The Site, Rebuilt

08 · Website

Home, Top to Bottom

Hero, services, the AI audit, process, why us, call to action, insights and footer.

08 · Website

Services & FAQ

All ten services as alternating rows, then the FAQ and the closing call to action.
